# Road to Legend v2

The campaign teaches the game through actual objectives, not a visible rank-progress grind. Rank thresholds/unlocks are unchanged; the new football names appear in the game guide. Existing six legacy chapters and their rewards remain preserved for the owner. Version 2 starts its own ordered claims and credits recorded actions/state where supported.

New equipment arrives unequipped, preserving the manager's existing selection. Already-owned reward gear pays its listed value instead. A full 30-owned squad reserves a reward player; no existing player is deleted and the reserved player does not expire or reroll. Capacity includes active listings. Claims/collection use actual ownership, player locks and receipt/claim uniqueness. Claiming does not award hidden rank progress.

No lesson requires another human online: alliances can be founded alone, ventures support owned players, transfer escrow can be learned by listing then withdrawing, and mail is introduced alongside the location board rather than requiring an unavailable recipient. Two-approach proof begins with the new audit records; old outcomes did not reliably record which approach was used. Replaying old action keys cannot invent approach usage.

Merchandise Runs is the bootlegging analogue: buy stock, carry it to another town and sell. It now sits in the Campaign menu. The quote guide compares all four towns and estimates a 20-unit loaded-trip net after one £50 journey. Getting to a source town may cost more, old stock can have another cost basis and prices reset at UTC midnight. The server still decides the actual location, quantity, quote, capacity and timer.
